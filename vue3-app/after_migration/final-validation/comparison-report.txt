BELOW THE 0.80 VISUAL THRESHOLD
-------------------------------
None - every pair is at or above 0.80 (lowest: campus/list.png at 0.8121).

The remaining deficit on the data-table screens sits entirely in the
edge-overlap (component) sub-score; their styling similarity is 0.993-0.995 and
layout similarity 0.987-0.993, i.e. no colour, font, spacing or dimension
regression. The edge metric reacts strongly to sub-pixel text anti-aliasing
differences between the webpack-built Vue 2 bundle and the Vite-built Vue 3
bundle rendering the same CSS. The pages are additionally verified
behaviourally by the UI behaviour tests CSET-01, CSET-02, CSET-03, CAMP-01 and
CAMP-03 (all passing) and by the Playwright flow specs C1/C5/C6/D1.

comparison is therefore like-for-like, but it does not exercise populated tables.
