BELOW THE 0.80 VISUAL THRESHOLD
-------------------------------
campus/list.png, campus/list-state-dropdown.png, campusset/list.png

All three are data-table screens. Their styling similarity is 0.987-0.989 and
layout similarity 0.981, i.e. no colour, font, spacing or dimension regression;
the deficit is entirely in the edge-overlap (component) sub-score, which reacts
strongly to sub-pixel text anti-aliasing differences between the webpack-built
Vue 2 bundle and the Vite-built Vue 3 bundle rendering the same CSS. The pages
are additionally verified behaviourally by the UI behaviour tests CSET-01,
CSET-02, CSET-03, CAMP-01 and CAMP-03 (all passing) and by the Playwright flow
specs C1/C5/C6/D1.

ENVIRONMENT NOTE
----------------

comparison is therefore like-for-like, but it does not exercise populated tables.
